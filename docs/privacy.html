Iron Ledger privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Iron Ledger is a personal workout tracker made by one person. It has no ads, does no tracking for advertising, and never sells or shares your data with data brokers.

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, settings, body measurements and progress photos are saved on your device. Progress photos are never uploaded except as described under "Photo analysis".

Your account (optional)

If you create an account, the app stores your username, a securely hashed password (never the password itself), and a copy of your workouts, settings and measurements on the Iron Ledger server (hosted on Cloudflare) so they sync between your devices. If you use the Friends features, the things you choose to share (such as your name, strength ranks and recent workouts) are visible to the friends you add. You can use the app as a guest without an account.

Apple Health

With your permission, the iPhone app reads weight, body fat, waist size, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps and active energy from Apple Health, and can save your workouts to Apple Health. Health data is used only to show your progress and calorie targets in the app. If you are signed in, these numbers are synced to your account like the rest of your data. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone else. You can turn access off at any time in the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Iron Ledger.

Photo analysis

If you tap "Analyze" on a progress photo, that one photo is sent to the Iron Ledger server's AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI) to produce feedback, and it is not stored afterwards.

Rest alerts

If you turn on rest alerts in the web app, your browser's push address is stored on the server so it can send a "Rest is over" notification. Turning alerts off stops them.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone is removed when you delete the app or clear it in Settings. To delete your account and its synced data, contact us using the link below.
