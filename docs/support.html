Iron Ledger support

Iron Ledger is a workout tracker: build workouts from the equipment you have, log sets, see your strength rank for each muscle group, and track runs, body measurements and meals.

Common questions

Rest alerts don't make a sound. With the iPhone on silent, notifications vibrate instead. In the iPhone Settings app → Sounds & Haptics, set Haptics to Always Play. Inside the app, the rest chime plays even on silent.

How do I sync between devices? Create an account in the app and sign in with the same username on each device.

Apple Health numbers are missing. Open the Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Iron Ledger and turn the categories on.
